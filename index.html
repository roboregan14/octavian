Octavian

Give them a champion.

Octavian is a workout app for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by Robert O'Regan. You log your lifting, record runs and walks, and a crowd gathers as you train. It has no account and sends nothing to a server of mine.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to roboregan14@outlook.com. TestFlight testers can also send a screenshot with a note from the TestFlight app.

Privacy

Your records stay on your phone and, if you allow it, in Apple Health. The full policy: privacy policy.
